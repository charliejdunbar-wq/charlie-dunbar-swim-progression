Performance points & IMX / IMR

USA Swimming Power Points and World Aquatics (formerly FINA) points are displayed separately. The scores compare performances across events; they are not qualifying-cut percentages.

Important: World Aquatics (formerly FINA) points use the 2026 men's long-course meter base times and official cubic formula. There is no official SCY/yards World Aquatics table, so SCY rows show “—”. USA Swimming Power Points are age-, event- and course-specific lookup values. Published matches are shown as sourced; same-age, same-event estimates are marked with “~”. Missing scores can be entered from the official Data Hub using the override box.

Important interpretation notes

Winter Juniors and Summer Junior Nationals are separate meet standards. Winter Juniors uses the 2026 Speedo Winter Junior Championships sheet; Summer Juniors uses the 2027 Speedo Junior National Championships sheet (the official meet title does not include “Summer”). These standards are national meet cuts, not age-group-specific entry guarantees; age eligibility and each qualifying window still apply.

The 2027 Toyota National Championships include two separate men’s standards: 18 & Under and 19 & Over. The 2026 Toyota U.S. Open benchmark shown is its published 18 & Under table; no 2027 U.S. Open standard was listed on USA Swimming’s standards page when this dashboard was updated. Olympic Trials is an elite LCM benchmark, not a forecast or age-based expectation.

MHSAA cuts shown are Lower Peninsula high-school qualifying standards, separated by Division 1, 2 and 3. They are not USA Swimming age-group standards. Long-distance paired standards are matched by course: 500 SCY ↔ 400 LCM, 1000 SCY ↔ 800 LCM, and 1650 SCY ↔ 1500 LCM. These are the meet’s published equivalent event pairs, not a mathematical conversion between course times.

No qualifying cut is loaded for 25-yard events or 100 IM where the listed standards do not include a matching event. Winter Juniors does not publish 50 Back, 50 Breast or 50 Fly individual standards. Relay cuts are not compared to individual swims.

The imported PB list is from the screenshots supplied in this chat. Some entries are dated 2021 or 2023, so the dashboard preserves their listed date; please update them when a newer PB exists.

USA Swimming Power Points are lookup scores, not a single public cubic formula. Exact published matches and same-age reference anchors are identified in the point table. A “~” score is an estimate calculated from a same-age, same-event, same-course reference point; it is not an official SWIMS result and can differ from the official table. Where a suitable reference is unavailable, the score stays blank until an official value is entered in the override field. World Aquatics/FINA points are calculated from the 2026 men’s LCM base times using floor(1000 × (base time ÷ swim time)³). There is no official SCY World Aquatics table; 100 IM is not included in the 2026 LCM base-time list.

IMX and IMR event sets follow USA Swimming’s 13–18 requirements. The displayed total is a live sum of the selected season’s event points; a complete, official score also depends on every required event being swum in a sanctioned meet in the same course and season. Any estimated Power Point makes the displayed total an estimate, not an official ranking.

Built for personal swim-progress tracking. Official qualifying standards can change by season; verify the meet packet, age-up rules, course, qualifying period and applicable division before using any time for meet entry. This dashboard is a planning aid, not an official eligibility determination.
